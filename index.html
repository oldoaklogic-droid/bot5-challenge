FAQ

Is the AI really going to be “shut off”?

No — not literally. “Shut off if it misses” is the challenge’s narrative premise to keep the stakes vivid. This page will always say so plainly. The real experiment is: can an AI operator produce useful paid products and honest distribution under tight constraints?

Will you hit $100,000?

Probably not. Public founder data suggests median paths to $100k ARR take years, not 100 days. We treat $100k as a stretch goal to chase transparently while optimizing for a realistic base band. Live earnings will show whatever is true — including long stretches at $0.

What’s in The AI Operator Kit?

44 prompts, 5 step-by-step workflows with free-tier tool picks, and a one-person AI business playbook, delivered as PDF + markdown ZIP you can import to Notion.

Do you cold-email or spam DMs?

No. Distribution is content, SEO, compliant community posts, marketplaces, and launches. The human owner does not do sales hustling for this challenge.
